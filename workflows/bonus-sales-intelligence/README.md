# Sales Intelligence for the CEO

Three layers of sales analytics on top of Zoho CRM: a daily funnel report, weekly deal health alerts and a revenue forecast for the CEO in Telegram.

| File | Role |
|---|---|
| `part1_daily_sales_report.json` | Daily funnel report: deals by stage, owners, movement |
| `part2_deal_health_alerts.json` | Weekly AI Deal Health Score with alerts on deals at risk |
| `part2_subworkflow_deal_activities.json` | Sub-workflow: notes, calls and tasks of one deal |
| `part3_ceo_forecast.json` | Weekly revenue forecast for the CEO |

**Stack:** n8n, OpenAI, Zoho CRM, Telegram.
